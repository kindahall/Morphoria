# Visual Style Lock

Morphoria must stay close to the existing visual references.

## Core Identity

- premium cartoon 3D platformer
- floating islands
- readable colored form zones
- golden faceted stars
- violet prism crystals
- blue crystal cages
- warm, magical, family-friendly adventure tone
- dark UI panels with bright colored accents

## Form Palettes

- Pierre / Rokko: ocher, stone gray, amber, heavy silhouette
- Feuille / Luma: leaf green, yellow, cyan wind, light winged silhouette
- Papier / Papyra: ivory, pale blue, violet runes, folded origami silhouette
- Ciseaux / Cizo: silver, blue, orange sparks, fast blade silhouette

## Do Not Drift Toward

- generic gray prototype-only look for longer than necessary
- realistic grim fantasy
- dark brown one-note palette
- direct resemblance to known platformer franchises
- UI that ignores the visual-card HUD

## Placeholder Rule

Placeholders are allowed only when they preserve:

- the correct color language
- the intended silhouette
- the gameplay readability
- the level layout from the visual cards

Every placeholder must be easy to replace later with GLB, texture, VFX, or animation assets.
